Who's highest this week

    Trophy case

    Set up your climb

    Every class has its own climber. Quiet work moves the climber up the cliff and finds treasures on the ledges. Too much noise makes them slip back to the last checkpoint. The class that climbs highest wins the week.

    Microphone

    Live level

    Calibration listens to 5 seconds of silence, then 5 seconds of normal talking, and sets the volume goals to fit your room.

    Short noises like a cough or a dropped pencil are ignored. The climber only reacts when the noise lasts.

    Height shown in

    Only changes how heights are shown. Progress is the same either way. The height-fact banners switch too.

    Falls

    Tough: the louder it gets, the worse the fall. Past the goal line the climber slips to the last checkpoint. Past the orange line he drops two checkpoints. Past the red line he drops three and loses a treasure. If it gets louder while he's hanging, he slips further. He never falls all the way to the bottom. Gentle: every fall is just to the last checkpoint.

    Climbing

    Height climbed per minute of quiet work. A checkpoint is clipped every 10 m (about 33 ft), and a ledge with something to collect turns up every couple of minutes.

    Saved progress

    Every class's height, trophies and wins save automatically in this browser and survive reloads and updates. Progress is kept per web address, so use the same link on the classroom computer every time. Save a backup file now and then to be extra safe, or to move to another computer.

    Developer mode

    For testing. Actions apply to the class showing now. Heights set here are real and get saved.
